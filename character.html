Character – Artist Makeup Paris

A semi-luxury, trend-setting cosmetics brand crafted for professional makeup artists and enthusiasts.

Brand Story

For over 20 years, a team of innovative young individuals have been working on perfecting an iconic range of techniques and formulas specially designed for Middle Eastern pro makeup artists. Character offers never-before-seen formulas made of the highest quality ingredients, achieving cult status in the Middle Eastern beauty market.

Rebranding & Influencer Power

In 2019, the brand rebranded 360 degrees with a new look and chic luxury products, supported heavily by influencer marketing. Character has collaborated with over 100 Middle Eastern makeup artists and influencers, growing strongly on online marketplaces like Boutiqaat, Faces.com, Brandatt, and more.

Manufacturing Power

Character Cosmetics is manufactured by Forever Makeup Trading LLC (established in 2008), producing face, eyes, lips, nails, brushes, sponges, and accessories. Products adhere to U.S. FDA rules for ingredients and labeling, with high-quality, FDA-approved ingredients.

Product Portfolio

Character offers a wide portfolio including:

  • Face: Silky Matte Foundation, Studio Focus Liquid Foundation, HD Coverage Concealer, Glow Kit, Rock Star Highlighter, etc.
  • Eyes: Brow Setter, Brow Palette, eyeliners, eyeshadow palettes (Modern, Glam Look, Majestic), mascaras.
  • Lips: Lip Plumper, Mini Lip Matte, My Style Lipstick, Creamy Matte Lipcolor, Fabulous Lip Crayon.
  • Brushes & Sponges: 15-piece and 23-piece brush sets, blending sponges, sponge cleansers, mixing plates.

Visual Merchandising & Presence

Character’s color scheme is champagne gold and white, with unified visual guidelines across territories. Flagship stores are located in The Dubai Mall, Doha, Dalma Mall Abu Dhabi, King Khalid Airport Riyadh, Boulevard Riyadh, Riyadh Park, Mall of Arabia Jeddah, and more.

Sales & Markets

Sales growth from 2018–2025 shows strong upward trends, with major markets including Saudi Arabia, UAE, Oman, Kuwait, Egypt, Iraq/Iran, Uzbekistan, Africa, Russia, Asia, and USA.

Partners

Retail partners include major malls and chains across GCC and beyond (The Dubai Mall, Mall of the Emirates, City Centre, Faces, Centrepoint, Riyadh Park, Mall of Arabia, KAFD, Mall of Dhahran, Duty Free, etc.), plus online partners like Faces, 6thStreet, SIVVI, Styli, Boutiqaat, Nykaa.
